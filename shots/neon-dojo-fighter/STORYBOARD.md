## HUD / systems
- **Health bars:** SF2 style, yellow on red, with a lagging white damage trail. Name plates, timer 99 counting down by beats, round pips.
- **Hits:** hit-stop 3–4 frames; spark sprites (small/large/block); screen shake 2–4 px for 6 frames; restraint (≤ 3 hits per bar).
- **Rounds:** ROUND 1 / FINAL ROUND / FIGHT! / K.O. / YOU WIN / PERFECT banners in pixel font.
- **VS screen:** diagonal split, portraits slide on the beat, VS slam with a flash.
- **Combo counter.** Super cut-ins with speed lines (existing cutins system).
- **Grounding:** a contact shadow on every fighter. QA per stage: feet within ±1 px of the ground.
