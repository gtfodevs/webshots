# Villain design brief: THE DEADSTAR COURT

**Role:** the doubters. They run the Deadstar Arena, a floating colosseum on the husk of a dead star. Each night they pick an underdog to "make the clown". Tonight they've picked Uno. They are kid-safe villains: smug, loud and theatrical, never gory. They lose by getting outplayed, and they end up dazed in a pile wearing their own clown-nose hologram.

**House style for all three:** polished chrome robots with glowing red eyes and headphone ears (the Progressive Slop brand). Their accent colours are violet-black and dim dead-star red, so they never read as Uno's purple-and-gold or Aura's crimson-and-cyan.

1. **NULLSTAR** (leader and ringmaster)
   - Build: very tall and thin, with long limbs.
   - Face: polished chrome with narrow glowing red eyes, a thin smirk line for a mouth, and headphone ears.
   - Chest: a dead-star core, a black sphere ringed by a dim red corona. It pulls light in, which is his gravity power.
   - Crown: jagged and made of broken black star shards.
   - Outfit: a long violet-black ringmaster tailcoat with a high collar.
   - Cane: tipped with a small dead-star orb.
   - Silhouette: a tall top-heavy spike with a long coat tail.
   - Personality: a showman and a smug announcer.
2. **GRAVEL** (the muscle)
   - Build: a hulking, hunched chrome brute about twice Uno's width.
   - Head: small, with red eyes and headphone ears.
   - Fists: huge, crusted with grey asteroid rock.
   - Shoulders: dented plates.
   - Silhouette: a wide boulder shape.
   - Personality: all bluff and loud.
3. **HECKLE** (twin hecklers)
   - Body: two small round flying chrome drones, each about the size of a head.
   - Face: red eyes and headphone ears.
   - Mouth: megaphone-shaped, firing magenta sonic rings and projecting a red holographic clown nose.
   - Movement: tiny hover jets underneath.
