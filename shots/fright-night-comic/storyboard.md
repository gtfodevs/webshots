# FRIGHT NIGHT storyboard (draft v1)
Stakes: Uno + Aura are trapped in a haunted mansion at 3AM; must reach the front door before the candles die. Villains: hollow-eyed shadow robots ("the Leaners"), a mirror-ghost, chrome wolves. Kid-safe eerie, no gore, no humans.
Palette: deep teal/black, sickly green, candle orange. Rain, fog, lightning flashes.
Intro: FRIGHT NIGHT dripping-ink logo slam with lightning.
1 Chorus "Fright night": storm, mansion on hill, lightning reveals red eyes in every window.
2 Aura (candle lantern) and Uno step inside; door SLAMS shut behind them (SFX).
3 "Deadbolt shaking": close on deadbolt rattling; scratch marks appear on the hall wall.
4 "Mud on the carpet": clawed footprints leading INTO the wall; Uno kneels, eyes flare.
5 "I don't like what I just saw": Aura's red eyes wide, lantern shaking.
6 "Figures in the hallway, leaning in": long hallway, tall thin shadow robots lean out of doorways.
7 Uno shoves Aura behind him, cyan magic flares; Leaners recoil (strike beat).
8 "Looking for the door": maze of identical doors, extreme dutch angle.
9 "Black cat screaming, clock stuck on three": chrome black cat hisses; grandfather clock frozen at 3:00.
10 "Every shadow got a name": shadows on the wall spell UNO and AURA.
11 "This whole house feels wrong": staircase twists upside down (Escher room).
12 "Wolves on the roof": chrome wolves silhouetted on the roof against the moon.
13 "Graveyard's gone": out the window, the graveyard sinks into fog.
14 Wolf crashes through the window: Uno uppercut mid-strike, KRAK! (fight beat-cut).
15 Aura cyan blast throws two wolves back, FWOOSH!
16 "Need out tonight": the pair sprint down the corridor, candles dying behind them one by one.
17 "Ghost in the mirror, moved twice": Aura's reflection moves when she doesn't.
18 Mirror-ghost reaches out of the glass; Aura shatters the mirror, SMASH!
19 "No sleep till dawn": ceiling bulges, dust falls, Uno looks up.
20 "I hear 'em at the ceiling": dozens of red eyes through cracks overhead.
21 "Doors start buckling, candles die": front door in sight, doors bowing, last candle snuffs out. Darkness, only their red eyes.
22 Uno and Aura blast the front door open together, BOOM!, and tumble out into the dawn.
23 Final chorus: sunrise over the hill, they laugh, safe... relief beat.
24 Slow push toward the mansion: one upstairs window, Aura's mirror-ghost waving goodbye.
25 CLIFFHANGER: Aura turns to Uno; in a close-up, her eyes flicker from red to the ghost's cold green. She smiles. Hard cut to black: "TO BE CONTINUED..."
End card.
